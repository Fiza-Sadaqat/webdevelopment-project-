Government College Women University Faisalabad (GCWUF)is a leading public-sector university dedicated to female empowerment and higher education in Faisalabad, Pakistan. Operating under the motto "Knowledge is Empowerment," the institution began its journey as an intermediate college in 1934 and was officially elevated to a full-fledged university on January 5, 2013. Currently located on Jaranwala Road near the historic Clock Tower, GCWUF serves over 11,000 students and features five major faculties: Arts & Humanities, Computing and Numerical Sciences, Management Sciences, Science and Technology, and Social Sciences. The university offers a comprehensive array of academic programs ranging from Associate Degrees (ADP) and BS degrees up to PhD programs. To accommodate its rapid growth, a massive new 396-acre campus project is under development at the Makkuana bypass
